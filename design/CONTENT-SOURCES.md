# Food-contact and environmental content

No client product composition, food-contact test reports, coatings, certifications or disposal tests have been supplied. The material-care section gives practical selection and use guidance rather than claiming bamboo provides inherent health benefits. The eco section addresses ordering quantities, material transparency and disposal routes rather than asserting a verified environmental advantage.

Sources reviewed:
- Food Standards Agency: https://www.food.gov.uk/news-alerts/news/updated-notice-on-plastic-containers-and-utensils-containing-bamboo (bamboo-plastic composites are distinct from actual bamboo products; do not assume food-contact suitability from appearance).
- US EPA: https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/composting (accepted materials differ between facilities).
- US EPA: https://www.epa.gov/greenerproducts/identifying-sustainable-food-service-and-food-service-ware (suitability of disposal choices depends on product properties and available collection).
- FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides (specific, supported environmental claims).

Verify the actual SKU materials, finishes, intended temperature and food uses, testing, sourcing, packaging and accepted disposal route before adding product-specific health or environmental benefits.
